GIS, Remote Sensing and AI-Assisted Cadastral-Level Crop Insurance Assessment: A Synthetic 100-Farmer Pilot Study

A Pilot Decision-Support Framework — not an automated insurance claim approval system. Core concept: “From Record-Based Assessment to Evidence-Based Parcel Intelligence.” This prototype does not claim to replace official PMFBY procedures; it demonstrates a technology-assisted decision-support and verification framework.

Research question: “Can cadastral-level integration of GIS, multi-temporal remote sensing and AI provide a more spatially explicit, transparent and evidence-supported approach to crop identification, cultivated-area estimation and crop-loss assessment compared with conventional record-based assessment?”
Demonstrates: Traditional Records → Technology-Assisted Assessment → Comparison → Insurance Decision Support.

⚠ Final Disclaimer

This is a Pilot Study/Proof-of-Concept based entirely on synthetic and simulated data. The farmer records, cadastral parcels, crop observations, remote-sensing observations, weather events, crop-loss estimates, insurance premiums and claim values are generated for research and demonstration purposes only. The system does not represent actual farmer records, official satellite observations, official crop-loss assessment or official PMFBY claim determination. Real-world deployment would require field validation, authorized datasets, applicable government protocols and regulatory approval.

What is real on this page: the Simrol village polygon (Survey of India via NWDP, vil_lgd 476504, Mhow block, Indore district, Madhya Pradesh) and its Census population/household fields; the per-crop yield baselines and PMFBY threshold yields derived from real DES (data.desagri.gov.in) Indore district records; and the notified PMFBY farmer premium-share caps. Everything else is synthetic or simulated and is labelled as such wherever it appears.
